## Longer description (if needed)

Undertow is a one-thumb endless swimmer. The water is split into horizontal
current bands that push you up or down, so you read the flow rather than just
tapping. You thread gaps between coral ledges, dodge drifting jellyfish, and
balance a draining air bar that only refills when you catch bubbles. Pearls
add to your score and coins.

Between runs, progress persists: coins buy skins, distance earns XP toward new
levels, and higher levels unlock themed areas (Coral Cove, Kelp Forest,
Midnight Trench, Frozen Reach, Volcanic Vents, The Abyss, and Aurora Deep),
each with its own palette and difficulty. Four daily missions and a five-minute
lucky wheel give reasons to return.
